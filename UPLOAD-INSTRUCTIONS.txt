DOG TAG DAY — WHAT TO UPLOAD

Do not upload the ZIP file itself.

1. Download DogTagDay_Official_Website.zip.
2. Open the ZIP.
3. Click "Extract all".
4. Open the extracted DogTagDay_Official_Website folder.
5. Copy these FOUR items into your local DogTagDay repository folder:
   - assets folder
   - index.html
   - styles.css
   - script.js
   (README.md may replace the existing README.md if you choose.)
6. Return to GitHub Desktop.
7. In Summary, type: Add official Dog Tag Day website
8. Click Commit to main.
9. Click Push origin.
