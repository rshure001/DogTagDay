With gratitude for those who served

To all the men and women who have served in the United States military, thank you for your immeasurable sacrifices. I am deeply proud of my nephew currently serving in the Navy, and a close friend who bravely served in Iraq and Afghanistan. While I am thankful for their safe return, I honor and remember those who made the ultimate sacrifice. May God bless all who are actively serving our nation today. Respectfully, Willie Beard III

— Willie Beard III · Family

A salute to everyone who served
