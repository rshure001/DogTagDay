An Open Letter to President Donald J. Trump

A respectful request to help make military service visible and bring national attention to Dog Tag Day on April 18.

Published September 1, 2026
President Donald J. Trump
The White House
1600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW
Washington, DC 20500

Dear President Trump,

For sixty years, I celebrated Veterans Day, yet too often I moved through daily life without seeing the veterans around me. They were there—in our neighborhoods, workplaces, stores, churches, and families—but their service had become invisible.

That is why I founded Dog Tag Day.

Every April 18, we ask veterans, active-duty service members, and military families to wear or display dog tags and share the stories behind them. We ask every American who sees those tags to pause, acknowledge the person wearing them, and say through a simple gesture: I see you.

Dog tags mean “I served.” Dog Tag Day means “You will not be invisible.”

This mission is nonpartisan. It belongs to every branch of service, every generation, and every American family touched by military service. It does not replace Veterans Day or Memorial Day. It creates one additional moment for identity, brotherhood, honor, and remembrance.

Mr. President, I respectfully ask you to help bring national attention to Dog Tag Day and to consider recognizing April 18 as an annual national observance. Your voice could help millions of Americans see the veterans who have been standing beside them all along.

Those who served should be seen, heard, and remembered—not only after they are gone, but while they are still here to tell their stories.

With respect,

Ronnie Shure
Founder & President
Dog Tag Day Foundation
DogTagDay.org

This open letter is a nonpartisan request for national recognition. It does not imply endorsement, affiliation, sponsorship, or a response from the President or the United States government.
