The Mission

Those stories deserve to be seen, heard, and remembered.

Dog Tag Day Foundation honors veterans by making service visible, preserving stories, connecting people to resources, and building community — not just on April 18, but every day of the year.

What We Do All Year

We show up. We listen. We stay.

WE LISTEN

We sit down, one-on-one, and hear the story.

WE PRESERVE STORIES

We help keep service histories and memories from disappearing.

WE CONNECT TO RESOURCES

We point veterans toward trusted help when they ask.

WE FOLLOW UP

We make sure the connection did not end with a phone number.

WE BUILD COMMUNITY

We bring veterans, families, businesses, schools, and communities together.

People Behind the Tags

Different moments. Different expressions. The same mission.

Veterans First

No costumes. No gimmicks. Service speaks for itself.
