What We Do All Year

We show up. We listen. We stay.

Dog Tag Day is not just a date. We listen to veterans, preserve stories with permission, connect people who ask for help to trusted resources, follow up, and build meaningful April 18 participation across America.

Dog Tag Day began with one realization: too many veterans become invisible once the uniform comes off.

Dog Tag Day exists so veterans can be seen while they are still here to tell us who they are, where they served, who they remember, and what their service meant.
