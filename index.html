DOG TAG DAY
APRIL 18

Once the uniform comes off, military service can become invisible. Dog Tag Day gives America one visible way to say: I see you.

Why Dog Tag Day Exists

Don’t let our veterans fade into the background.

Millions of veterans live, work, shop, raise families, and stand beside us every day. Often, we never know they served. On April 18, veterans who choose to participate wear or display their military dog tags. The tags say, “I served.” America answers, “I see you.”

17.2 MILLIONLIVING U.S. VETERANS
What You Do on April 18

Four simple actions.

1

Wear the tags

If you served, put on or display your military dog tags.

2

Honor someone

Families may display the tags of someone they love or remember.

3

Acknowledge them

A nod, handshake, fist to the heart, or simply: “I see you.”

4

Share the story

With permission, share the person and the story behind the tags.

We show up. We listen.
What We Do All Year

Visibility. Stories. Connection.

Preserve Stories

Record and preserve veteran stories, photographs, and service histories with permission.

Connect Veterans

When a veteran asks for help, connect them with trusted local and national resources.

Build April 18

Help communities, schools, employers, veterans groups, and civic organizations participate meaningfully.

Service. Sacrifice. Courage. Brotherhood. Family. The people behind the tags deserve to be remembered.
