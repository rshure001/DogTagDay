APRIL 18 • IDENTITY • BROTHERHOOD • HONOR

Our Mission

Don’t let our veterans fade into the background.

Dog Tag Day makes military service visible. On April 18, veterans and service members are invited to wear or display their dog tags. Families may wear tags in honor or memory of someone they love.

Those stories deserve to be seen, heard, and remembered.

What You Can Do

One line. One action at a time.

Wear Your Tags

Put on your dog tags. Let them say, “I served.”

Acknowledge One

See the tags. Acknowledge the person — a nod, handshake, fist to the heart, or simply, “I see you.”

Tell the Story

With permission, take a picture or video and share the story behind the tags.
