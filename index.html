Identity • Brotherhood • Honor

Veterans should not become invisible.

Once the uniform comes off, military service can become invisible. Dog Tag Day gives America one visible way to see the veteran, hear the story, and remember the service.

What We Do All Year

We show up. We listen. We stay.

Dog Tag Day is more than one day. We listen to veterans, preserve stories with permission, help connect people to trusted resources, follow up, and build meaningful April 18 participation across America.

Preserve stories

Record veteran stories and service histories with permission.

Connect resources

Guide veterans who ask for help toward trusted local resources.

Follow up

Reconnect after referrals and events so veterans are not left behind.

Build April 18

Help communities participate meaningfully across America.
