Our Mission

Every story deserves dignity.

Dog Tag Day honors veterans, active-duty service members, military families, and every life represented by military dog tags.

Some stories are joyful. Some are painful. Some remain private. No one is required to tell a story before they are ready. Wearing the tags is enough.

Dog Tag Day is not about celebrating war. It is about recognizing service, preserving memory, and reminding people that they are seen.

Why April 18?

Spring.

April 18 was chosen because spring represents renewal, hope, and new beginnings. Dog Tag Day renews our connection with those who served and keeps their stories alive.

One wave. One nod. One story.

The Annual Tradition

Las Vegas: the birthplace of Dog Tag Day.

Every April 18, wear a white T-shirt and military dog tags. Recognize another person wearing theirs with a wave, a nod, a handshake, or a respectful question: “Where did you serve?”

Wear

Wear military dog tags proudly. Supporters may stand beside those who do.

Connect

Offer a wave, nod, handshake, or respectful acknowledgment.

Remember

Honor those who served, military families, and those who never came home.

The Founder’s Vision
