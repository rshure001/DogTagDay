DOG TAG DAY
APRIL 18

Once the uniform comes off, military service can become invisible. Dog Tag Day gives America one visible way to say: I see you.

Why It Matters

Veterans should not become invisible.

17.2M
LIVING U.S. VETERANS

They live, work, shop, raise families, and stand beside us every day. Often, we never know they served.

April 18

Four simple actions.

SWIPE SIDE TO SIDE →
1

Wear the tags

If you served, wear or display your military dog tags.

2

Honor someone

Families may display the tags of someone they love or remember.

3

Acknowledge one

A nod, handshake, fist to the heart, or simply “I see you.”

4

Share the story

With permission, tell the story behind the tags.
