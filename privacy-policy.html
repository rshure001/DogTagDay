Privacy Policy

Dog Tag Day Foundation respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit DogTagDay.org, contact the foundation, share a story, or interact with our services.

Information you provide

If you voluntarily submit a contact message, veteran story, partnership inquiry, or other form, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, and any other information you include.

How information is used

We use submitted information to respond to requests, review stories, communicate about Dog Tag Day activities, operate the foundation, and provide requested support or information. We do not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information through this website unless it is necessary for a specific request.

Third-party services

Some website functions may use third-party services such as hosting, forms, maps, payment processing, analytics, or social-media integrations. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Cookies and technical information

The website may use ordinary technical information needed to operate the site. Some third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Sharing

Dog Tag Day Foundation does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate a requested website function or respond to a request, or when required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you previously submitted by contacting the foundation. We may need to retain certain records when required for legal, accounting, security, or operational reasons.
