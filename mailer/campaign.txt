I’m Ronnie Shure, Founder & CEO of Dog Tag Day Foundation. The upcoming Dog Tag Day observance is April 18, 2027, a day of visible recognition for veterans, service members, and their families. Our current outreach is building awareness and partnerships in preparation for that observance.

The idea is simple: veterans and active-duty service members may wear their dog tags, and families may wear or carry the tags of loved ones who served. Participation is voluntary. The purpose is visibility, recognition, and connection — a simple way of saying, “I see you.”

I would welcome a conversation about how Dog Tag Day Foundation can help your work and how we might introduce April 18, 2027 to veterans and families.

Thank you for the work you do for our veteran community.
