User Data Deletion Instructions

Dog Tag Day Foundation respects requests to delete personal information submitted through DogTagDay.org or connected foundation services.

How to request deletion

Email rshure001@yahoo.com with the subject Dog Tag Day Data Deletion Request.

Tell us the email address or other identifying information associated with the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information.

What we will do

We will review the request, verify that we can identify the relevant information, and delete or de-identify personal information we control when appropriate. Some information may need to be retained when required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or for accounting and legal records.

Meta/Facebook-related requests

If you used a Meta/Facebook-connected feature and want information associated with that connection deleted, include that fact in your request so we can identify the relevant records.
